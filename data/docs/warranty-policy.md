# Warranty and Repairs

All electronic products come with a 2-year limited warranty that starts on the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or damage caused by unauthorized repairs. Batteries are covered for 1 year only, because they wear out with use.

To make a warranty claim, open a ticket in your account under Support and attach your proof of purchase and a short video showing the problem. Our team reviews claims within 3 business days.

Approved claims are resolved by repair or replacement, at our choice. Repairs usually take 10 to 15 business days. During a repair, we do not provide loaner devices.

Extended warranty can be bought within 30 days of delivery. It adds 1 extra year of coverage for 15 percent of the product price. The extended warranty can be transferred to a new owner if the product is sold.
