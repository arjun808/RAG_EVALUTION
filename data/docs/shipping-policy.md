# Shipping Policy

We ship orders from our warehouse in Rotterdam. Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff are dispatched the next business day.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and costs 4.90 euros. Orders above 60 euros qualify for free standard shipping. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12.50 euros.

International orders are shipped with tracked delivery only. Delivery to countries outside the European Union takes 7 to 14 business days, and the customer is responsible for any import duties and taxes charged at the border.

After dispatch, you receive an email with a tracking number. If a parcel has not arrived 10 business days after dispatch, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

We do not ship to PO boxes or to freight forwarders. Orders to these addresses are cancelled and refunded automatically.
