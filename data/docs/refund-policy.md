# Refund Policy

Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The 30-day window starts on the delivery date shown in the tracking information, not the order date.

To start a return, contact support with your order number and the reason for the return. Support will email a prepaid return label within 1 business day. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the returned item. Refunds paid by gift card are returned as store credit.

Some items cannot be returned. Opened software, personalized products, and perishable goods are final sale. Sale items marked "clearance" can only be exchanged, not refunded.

If an item arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, including the original shipping fee.
